About Gold

Welcome to Gold Family Restaurant, your destination for authentic family dining in Pudukkottai. With a 4.4-star rating and over 397 reviews, we pride ourselves on delivering exceptional cuisine and outstanding service.

Our diverse menu features everything from traditional Tandoori chicken and mouth-watering Mutton Biryani to our signature Egyptian BBQ. We believe in using fresh ingredients and time-tested recipes to create unforgettable dining experiences.

  • Family-friendly atmosphere
  • Wide variety of cuisines
  • Quick & friendly service
  • Ample parking available
