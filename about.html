About Koodoo

Our Story & Values

Welcome to Koodoo Family Restaurant

Since our opening, Koodoo has been Pudukkottai's destination for authentic, delicious food and warm hospitality. With a proud 4.4-star rating from over 397 satisfied customers, we continue to serve our community with excellence.

Our journey began with a simple mission: to bring families together through great food and memorable dining experiences. Located at Hotel Sivalaya, opposite RKP Theatre, we've built a reputation for quality, freshness, and innovation in every dish we serve.

What sets us apart is our commitment to using premium ingredients, traditional cooking methods, and modern culinary techniques to create a diverse menu that appeals to every palate - from our signature Mutton Biryani to our unique Egyptian BBQ.

We take pride in offering not just food, but an experience - a place where families laugh, celebrate, and create memories together.

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To serve authentic, high-quality cuisine in a warm and welcoming environment, making every dining experience memorable for our guests. We strive to maintain the highest standards of food quality, hygiene, and customer service while celebrating the rich culinary traditions of our region.

Our Vision

To become Pudukkottai's most trusted and beloved family restaurant, known for our commitment to excellence, innovation, and creating a gathering place where families and friends can connect over delicious food and quality time together.
